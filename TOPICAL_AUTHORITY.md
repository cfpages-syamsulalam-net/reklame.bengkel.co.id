# Topical Authority — reklame.bengkel.co.id

## Role and boundary

`reklame.bengkel.co.id` is a signage and outdoor-media knowledge/commercial-support property. It helps business owners, property teams, marketers, and procurement teams choose, specify, permit, install, inspect, and maintain physical and digital signage. Transactional routes own quotation, availability, fabrication, installation, and conversion. Exact structural, electrical, wind, fire, traffic, and permit requirements remain project-specific evidence gates.

## Context used

- Homepage promise: creative, professional, high-quality advertising media.
- Named services: baliho, billboard, branding mobil, huruf timbul, neon box, neon flex, papan nama, running text, totem/pole sign, videotron, and permit/tax assistance.
- Repository: 170 tracked files, one sitemap with 23 entries, one root homepage, and compact product/service route families.

## Ignored template noise

## Internal-link rule

Each article links to its parent hub. Media-selection pages link to the relevant product hub, then to site survey, design, fabrication, permit, and lifecycle pages. Diagnostic pages link to stop-use/escalation guidance and the relevant repair-or-replacement decision. Commercial routes receive links only after requirements are clear.
